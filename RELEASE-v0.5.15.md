# The Smoke v0.5.15 — Public beta radio safety

This is an incremental release built from the v0.5.14 full-system ZIP.

- The six original playable radio presets remain. CodeSouth and Esencia Urbana are retired, including their optional campaign contact sites and historical preset IDs in existing worlds.
- The player-facing radio no longer includes clickable websites, directory, chat, WhatsApp, text, telephone or email links. **Message Studio** and **Request Airtime** send private, GM-moderated in-game messages.
- The GM radio keeps its real studio contact controls, and gains a **GM Studio Inbox** to privately answer, decline, air an in-game announcement immediately, or schedule it in Banana Watch minutes.
- Radio-war missions and fictional relay sites for the six original stations remain untouched. The old two optional contact markers are removed only if they were system-created.
- Existing token drag, map movement, eight-hour rest, spray shops, crew progression, graffiti and original campaign systems are retained.

The new console does not and cannot post announcements to actual radio stations. Their stream content, trademarks and websites are independently controlled. See `BETA-LEGAL-CHECKLIST.md`. This is a GitHub/Forge update candidate with automated tests, not a claim that the broadcasters have authorised any specific use.

**Live acceptance still required** with two Forge accounts, a fresh world, and an upgraded existing world; test real audio availability, radio GUI permissions, chat whisper privacy, GM reply/announcement, scheduled release after rest, plus ordinary movement/chase/mission flow.

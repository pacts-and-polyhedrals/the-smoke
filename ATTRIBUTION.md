# Project sources

- The Smoke work in progress, supplied by the user: the 14 October and updated 15 October 2026 packages. The 15 October map assets and map metadata are the final base.
- The Smoke v0.1.3 (15 October 2026, supplied by the user) and The Smoke v0.2.0 (15 October 2026): the two builds combined in v0.2.1.
- `altered-carbon-rpg-v2.7.5.zip`, supplied by the user: implementation reference for vehicle boarding, map transit, LevelDB packing and Foundry interface conventions.
- `covenant-dice-v1.3.7.zip`, supplied by the user: Covenant dice, difficulty and rules implementation reference.
- New interface, integration repairs, starter writing, item library, journals, compendium build and tests were authored for this requested project.

The Covenant reference's rights notice is retained as `LICENSE-COVENANT.txt`. This build does not broaden permissions in the supplied source material or claim sole authorship of it. No Foundry proprietary source files or complete commercial reference rulebooks are bundled.

Item icons are drawn from [game-icons.net](https://game-icons.net) glyphs by Lorc, Delapouite and contributors (CC BY 3.0), via the react-icons package, recoloured into The Smoke's house style.

Live radio presets link to independent broadcasters and their radio.net directory pages; the system bundles no broadcasts, recordings or player code. See RADIO.md.

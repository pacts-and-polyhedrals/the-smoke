# The Smoke · Public beta publication checklist (author review required)

## Suggested disclaimer — edit and approve yourself before publishing

**The Smoke is an independently developed, fictional tabletop roleplaying game by Pacts & Polyhedrals.** Any real radio stations named in the radio tuner are referenced solely to identify their independently operated live broadcasts. They do not sponsor, endorse, collaborate on, or participate in the development of The Smoke unless an explicit partnership is separately disclosed. All in-game station contacts, rivalries, storylines, studio messages and announcements are fictional and must not be understood as describing the activities of real radio broadcasters or their staff. In-game studio messages remain within the game and do not contact the actual stations. Third-party audio streams are controlled by their respective operators and may be withdrawn or changed without notice. No third-party broadcast recordings are supplied with this system. All trademarks and programme content remain the property of their respective owners.

This is suggested wording, **not permission or a legal licence**. Have a qualified adviser review the intended public use, especially the real station names in crime-themed fictional missions and any artwork depicting real marks.

## Confirm before GitHub beta

- [ ] Inspect source and bundled assets for copyrighted music, recordings, broadcaster logos, artwork, book text, and third-party software. Retain appropriate licence notices and obtain permissions where required.
- [ ] Verify each direct radio URL against the operator's terms of use and ensure users only connect to their own permitted streams. Do not rebroadcast or proxy music yourself.
- [ ] Keep all player studio messages and shout-outs in-world with GM approval; confirm no player-only controls open real studio contact links.
- [ ] Clearly mark fictional radio-war contacts as invented. Consider replacing real station names with fictional ones in radio-war missions for lower legal and reputational risk.
- [ ] Check data/privacy expectations for multiplayer beta testers: message retention, chat logs, backups, retention period and who can read private requests.
- [ ] Test live Forge with a player account, GM account, an existing world and a new world. Ensure GM-only permissions are enforced and Ban­ana Watch triggers scheduled announcements after rest/time jumps.
- [ ] Check Foundry's official package listing rules. Under the March 2026 AI Content Policy, newly submitted official packages must use human-created *prepared* prose/art/marketing, while AI-assisted code requires author understanding and maintenance. Review existing website/logo assets, generated art, descriptions and campaign writing for compliance.

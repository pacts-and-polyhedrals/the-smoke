# Changelog
## 0.4.0 — every campaign venue mapped, and the cast (15 Oct 2026)
- **160 venue interiors**, one scene each, from the *NEED MAP Register*: six keyed rooms (zone plaques carry the register's codes, HB1.1 … HB1.6), connected as the register says (1–2, 2–3, 2–4, 3–5, 4–5, 5–6, 1–6) with a door on every connection, the public entry at room 1 and the loading or secondary arrival at room 6, each with its own way out. Sizes follow each brief (32×24 squares; 40×28 sports venues; 44×28 depots and the produce hall; 48×32 the stadiums and Westfield; 36×16 Barge East). Layouts are drawn per venue, so no two pubs are alike, and every room is furnished for what it is — nave and altar, bar and snug, cellar barrels, stalls, brewing tanks, print presses, a velodrome, a lido, a boxing ring, a mill wheel, a moored barge. Vehicles are never drawn: yards have marked loading and lorry bays for vehicle tokens.
- **51 approach maps** for the venues off the drawn roads: a park or cemetery path, a square, a plaza, a side street of terraces, a market or depot gate, a yard under the railway arches, a stadium forecourt, a towpath or riverside. Each has the street it leaves (and the way back), an **ENTER** pin at each venue's front door and a **SERVICE** pin at its back way in; six serve two venues.
- **152 entrance pins on the 20 street maps** (one per street-front venue and per approach), drawn as the street maps draw doors and placed on the road beside each venue's anchor, clear of exits, other doors and each other. Walk in and the venue (or approach) is imported from the new **London Venues** compendium on the spot and filed under *The Smoke — Venues*, one folder per district (each venue followed by its approach). Coming out you stand beside the pin. Vehicles park outside. *Import all maps* still brings only the streets and the four core interiors.
- **Mission Board:** every place a mission visits is now MAPPED; a map button beside each opens (and imports) that venue, **Prepare this mission's maps** imports them all with their approaches, and **Directions** takes the sat nav to the venue's entrance. Walking into a mission's venue (or its approach) ticks its objective, as reaching the district already did.
- **Sat nav:** all 160 venues in the destination list, filed by district; a character inside a venue or on its approach is placed at the street entrance; every venue routes from anywhere, on foot and by road.
- **The campaign cast:** 115 named people from the plan and the register — the twelve faction patrons and their people (Dee Hassan, Tessa "Moth" Price, Gita Mercer, Evie Slate, Frankie Wu, Len Fisk, Martin Rusk…), the mission givers and the contact at every venue. 108 new top-down tokens in faction colours and NPC actors (stats for their trade, role, health, a GM biography with their faction, venues and hooks) in the new **Campaign Cast** compendium; the opening's own people keep their actors. Mission givers now show their portraits; the Mission Board lists who each mission involves, and **Bring in the cast** imports them into *The Smoke — Campaign Cast*, filed by faction (`game.smoke.cast.import(keys)`).
- **Journals:** the *Venue Register* shows each venue's map, rooms, entrance and approach (and each approach map); plan text and place tags say MAPPED; the campaign book's map chapter records that every map is drawn (only the two optional extras remain NEED MAP).
- **Upgrading worlds:** guide pages a world still has exactly as an earlier release shipped them are now refreshed on upgrade (pages carry a fingerprint, and `data/guide-history.json` knows the 0.2.1–0.3.0 pages); pages the GM has written in are left alone. Street scenes pick up the new pins on the GM's next login.
- Suites: new `tests/venues.test.mjs` (every room, link, door, landing and pin; imports and folders; destinations, parking and way-out transit; mission stops; the cast and its import; the guide refresh); the sat nav suite covers venue destinations, approach doors and mission targets.
## 0.3.0 — the Sandbox Campaign (15 Oct 2026)
- **113 new missions** from the campaign plan, in the style of the opening: twelve faction careers of eight commissions (The Chainring, Dead Air Collective, the Lea Boys, the Velvet Circuit, the Towpath Union, the Glasshouse Exchange, the Backroom Bookmakers, the Night Market, Borough Mutual, the Lantern Desk, Meridian Renewal, the Red Thread) and twenty independent local cases, one per district. Each has a giver, a hook, objectives (the ones that reach a place tick themselves), a crew fee, XP, Renown, scenes, pressure, the choice and what follows. CH1–CH3 are Missions 1–3 of *On Your Bike*, which gain the Chainring's Renown and the plan's expanded scenes.
- **Renown, Influence and stance per faction:** a commission opens when the crew's Renown with that faction reaches its offer gate (0, 1, 3, 5, 8, 11, 15, 20); MISSION PASSED splits the crew fee, awards Renown (a local case pays one of its two factions — the GM picks) and restores Influence up to the rank's cap (Unproven 1 … Pillar 6). The card says what opened.
- **Mission Board:** pick the opening, a career or the local cases at the top of the list; faction colours and emblems; places with MAPPED / NEED MAP tags that open the Venue Register; GM notes with the pressure, the choice, what follows, Quiet Ledger evidence and clickable next jobs; a **Factions & Renown** panel to adjust Renown, Influence and stance; **Directions** puts the sat nav on the mission's next place (also on the Blower's mission card), moving on as objectives tick.
- **15 GM journals:** *The Smoke — Sandbox Campaign* (the expanded sandbox, Renown and interior access, running the open city, the Quiet Ledger truth and its six clue routes, starting nights and personal stakes for the four pregens, the final night and its endings, faction relationships, map production, GM records); twelve *Faction* journals (profile, rank benefits, Influence, places that open, the career, and every commission page); *Local Cases*; and the *London Interiors — Venue Register* (160 proposed interiors by district with contacts, room keys, street-map anchors and access, plus the mapped interiors and street scenes). 3,600+ cross-links between pages.
- Twelve faction emblems (and one for the local cases).

## 0.2.4 — the Blower's sat nav (15 Oct 2026)
- **Sat nav:** a job's **Directions** (Blower) now opens a sat nav in the bottom-right corner of the screen, on the London overview map: your route along the real roads, the next turn with its distance and a "then" for the one after, the road you're on, distance left and the time on foot, by bike or by car. It re-routes every time your token (or the motor you're in) moves, crosses from map to map with you and names the exit to leave by, and says when you've arrived.
- **Follows the job:** pick-up then drop-off for fares and deliveries, each bus stop in turn, the patient then A&E, the suspect then custody, the nearest of Pat's tip-offs (then The Arches once you're riding one), and a stolen car or suspect live once you're chasing it. When the job ends, so does the route.
- **Go anywhere:** **Sat nav** on the Blower, Alt+N or the scene-control button picks from your jobs, The Arches and your flat, services (A&E, police stations, fire stations, the resprays, the clothes markets, the taxi rank), landmarks and every district.
- **On the map too:** the route is drawn on the street map you're looking at (and across the London overview), for your eyes only. Zoom, show the whole route, north-up or heading-up; tap the instruction bar to fold the map away. Miles and yards by default, or metric (Settings). The Blower shows the destination and distance left, live, with Show / hide, Somewhere else and End route.
- One road network for all 20 street maps, built from the traced roads (crossings, side roads that stop just short of a main road, two stray pieces bridged); every destination reaches every other, by car and on foot. New suite: `tests/satnav.test.mjs`.
## 0.2.3 — Studio chat inside Foundry (15 Oct 2026)
- **Studio chat:** where a station has its own chat room or message box, **Message the studio** now opens it in a Foundry window, so players post through the station's own system without leaving the game. The message is copied as the window opens (paste it in and post); a second press brings the same window forward with the new message rather than reloading the chat; **Copy again**, **Reload** and **Open on their site** sit in the window.
- Supplied for Rude FM (their Chatroll room), Vision Radio UK (their Minnit chatbox) and Point Blank FM (the homepage "Message the Studio" box, shown if their site allows being framed). WhatsApp, text, email and call stay as they were for the other stations.
- None of the stations publishes a messaging API, and browsers stop one site posting into another's for a visitor, so nothing is sent on a player's behalf: they type into the real chat. RADIO.md explains why.
- The GM sets a station's **Studio chat** address (HTTPS, as their site embeds it) in **Edit presets**; presets saved earlier gain the supplied chats.

The GM edits them in **Edit presets**; presets saved earlier keep the supplied contacts.

## 0.2.1 — both branches in one (15 Oct 2026)
The v0.1.3 work (live radio, local services, recovery, advances, hardened requests, job and vehicle fixes, the numbered guides) and the v0.2.0 build (GM Screen, Combat Console, shops, missions, Character Creator and levelling, the item library, the books), combined. Nothing from either is dropped; where both did the same job, one version now carries both sets of features.
- **Live pirate radio (0.1.3):** six presets (Point Blank FM, Rude FM, Vision Radio UK, Flex FM, Supreme FM, Radio Caroline UK) in the Blower, the scene-control radio and **Car radio** on a vehicle's right-click; the driver tunes for everyone aboard; per-player volume and mute; GM preset editor (Settings → Edit radio stations); Retry/Resume; no autoplay after a reload. See RADIO.md.
- **Local services (0.1.3), shared with the Shop window (0.2.0):** respray & new plates £100 (Heat cleared, ANPR marker gone, still stolen for the fence), a change of clothes £25 (−1 star) at the market stalls, garage repairs £10 per missing Body (a flat tyre counts as six) at the respray shops, Bow Breakers and The Arches. Prices are world settings; police sight and the quoted price are checked; the stash is protected. One set of rules behind *Local services* (sheet, token HUD, vehicle menu) and the Shop window's Services list.
- **Recovery:** first aid (sheet button: Savvy, once per injury, +2 Health, Bleeding and Hurt stop) alongside the first-aid kit, bandages and painkillers; **Rest** at the flat or a safehouse is one rule for the sheet button and the flat's "Kip": full Health and Hustle, short-term conditions cleared, two stars or less of Heat dies down; not while Bleeding, at three stars or more, or with police in sight. A&E sets a **Broken** limb in a cast: it still takes a week (0.1.2 rule).
- **Growth:** GM-awarded **advances** (Street Control, or the GM Screen's Rewards) spent on the sheet's Rep tab within the Rep stage's cap, alongside XP levelling and the Level Up wizard. The quick builder (one d8, three d6, five d4) sits beside the full Character Creator.
- **Combat:** hits now offer **Take hit** or **Dodge** (the target's reaction: Moves against the attack's total) and resolve once; the weapon's own effect lands on a hit and the GM may add one listed condition (Settings → *Combat: apply damage automatically* switches to instant hits). The Combat Console's round end also resets reactions and runs the Down countdown (Stay Conscious each round; three rounds, or a Proper Mess, is Wasted). Fights on the core tracker get 0.1.2's turn clock: Bleeding at turn end, Stunned expiry, reaction reset, the Down countdown. Only the worn armour counts; Bruised stacks into Hurt; the attack, ammo, equip and adjacency checks of 0.1.2 apply to quick attacks.
- **Requests and rolls (0.1.2):** leader actions run one at a time and reply to the asking player (errors come back to them); rolls run on the GM so shared Hustle and the last round cannot be spent twice; the target is fixed before Hustle and perks; Down characters can only roll to stay conscious; written-off vehicles cannot be driven.
- **Jobs, vehicles, maps (0.1.2/0.1.3):** cross-map job references, full ambulance/cage checks, call ownership, double acceptance, paused timestamps (offers, deadlines, patient decline), target cleanup protection; the driver gets their own copy of a vehicle; footpath exits, the five-star roadblock before crossing, the traced road network for traffic and police.
- **Content:** 171 items — 133 from 0.2.0 plus 38 from 0.1.3 (Compact Pistol, ARV Carbine, Conducted-Energy Device, Police Baton, Ballistic Vest, Handcuffs, Service Radio, the Paramedic Bag and 16 perks among them), all with house-style icons and stocked where they would be sold; 31 conditions; 72 actors, with the 0.1.3 GM notes and duty kit added to the named NPCs; **twelve guides, 107 pages**: 0.1.3's seven numbered quick guides (updated where the merge changed a rule) and 0.2.0's Player's Handbook, GM's Book, London Guide, Street Papers and the 13-page missions guide. GM guides and missions stay private.
- **Setup and upgrades:** *Street Control → Set up the world* (or the welcome card) assigns the pregens to players and imports everything; kit is filed by type in *The Smoke — Kit*. Worlds from 0.1.x (either branch) and 0.2.0 upgrade on the GM's next login, keeping characters, notes, ownership and positions.
- **Interface:** the Blower carries the radio, the roll each job stage needs, Directions, Travel, available shifts and the guide, with in-place countdowns; Street Control adds world setup, diagnostics, guides, the atlas, Heat by number, advances and job vehicles; the GM Screen's Tools add setup, radio, services, first aid, rest, advances, job vehicles and diagnostics; Alt+B opens the Blower.
- Not carried over: 0.1.3's own sheet, Blower and Street Control templates (their features now live in the 0.2.0 windows above), its `items.json` (fully contained in the library) and four unused alternate pregen records in its NPC catalogue.
